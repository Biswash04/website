Courses We Offer

Gandaki Boarding School (GBS) offers a range of educational programs, including primary and secondary education (Grades 4-12), +2 Science, and Bachelor of Engineering (BE) in Software and Computer Engineering through its sister organization, Gandaki College of Engineering and Science (GCES).

Basic Level

Gandaki Boarding School (GBS), located in Pokhara, Nepal, offers a comprehensive education for basic level

Higher studies

GBS offers Plus Two programs in Science with affiliation to the National Education Board (NEB) and the Cambridge International A-Level program.

Bachelor's Degrees

Gandaki College of Engineering and Sciences (GCES), a sister organization of GBS, offers Bachelor of Engineering (BE) courses in Software Engineering with affiliation to Pokhara University.

Our Global Status

The school is a multi-lingual, multi-cultural and multi-ethnic educational institution that incorporates all castes, cultures, religions, values, and languages. It features an inquiry based, concept driven curriculum that provides a well-rounded, student centred programme. The language of instruction is English.

Our Facilities

Gandaki Boarding School in Pokhara offers a range of facilities for its students, including a health center, transportation, and a well-equipped library. The school also provides access to science laboratories, a dining hall for healthy meals, and a gymnasium for indoor activities. Furthermore, they offer a swimming pool and various clubs and extracurricular activities.

World Class Library

Gandaki Boarding School have more than 14000 books from different category and other national and international magazine , which are easily accessible to our student and staff.

Largest Playground

Gandaki Boarding School in Pokhara has a spacious playground forsports and other outdoor activities, as well as a gymnasium hall for indoor games.

Tasty And Healty Food

Gandaki Boarding School has a big dining hall where students eat their daily meals. It serves healthy and balanced food to support students living on campus.
